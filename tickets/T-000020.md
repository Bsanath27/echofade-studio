## Goal
Strip playlist and mix query parameters from watch links before passing them to yt-dlp, ensuring only the specific video requested is processed rather than looping through and downloading the entire auto-generated YouTube playlist.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Create a `clean_youtube_url` helper in `downloader.py` that parses incoming URLs.
- [x] Strip `list`, `index`, and other non-video parameters from both `youtu.be` and `youtube.com` links.
- [x] Apply sanitation to all entry points: `download_audio`, `download_media`, and `extract_media_info`.
