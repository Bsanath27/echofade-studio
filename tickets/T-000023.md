## Goal
Sanitize literal curly braces `{` and `}` in track lyrics prior to compiling `.ass` subtitle files. This prevents libass from misinterpreting text as override formatting tags (e.g. `\pos` or `\fad`), which would swallow words or crash the filter block.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Replace `{` with `(` and `}` with `)` in all lyric lines, stack highlights, and word timing segments in `ffmpeg_engine.py`.
- [x] Retain original timing timestamps while safeguarding formatting parsing integrity.
