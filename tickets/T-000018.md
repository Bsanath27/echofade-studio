## Goal
Fix rotoscoping issues where subsequent clicks (second click onwards) failed to add regions of the subject that the initial AI segmentation (U2-Net) missed. Ensure click coordinates map correctly to original-size image inputs without scaling offset, and update GrabCut baseline initialization from definite to probable background/foreground.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Prevent BFS from skipping positive clicks falling on background pixels (`binary == 0`).
- [x] Force-seed a local disk in `selected_mask` around positive clicks on background pixels so the mask expands to missed areas.
- [x] Set the initial U2-Net trimap labels in GrabCut's mask to probable background/foreground (`cv2.GC_PR_BGD`, `cv2.GC_PR_FGD`) instead of definite labels (`cv2.GC_BGD`, `cv2.GC_FGD`).
- [x] Reserve definite labels (`cv2.GC_BGD` and `cv2.GC_FGD`) strictly for the user's manual click coordinates to lock and force corrections.
